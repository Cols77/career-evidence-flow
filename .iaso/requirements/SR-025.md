title: Resume summary sentences are linked
statement: When drafting a resume summary, each sentence that follows from the previous one in time, cause
  or emphasis opens with a connecting phrase stating that relation, and the resume reviewer reports unlinked
  sentences that could be linked, or an unsupported connector, as a medium voice finding
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: a summary of four separate statements about a career, a current role, partner work and a personal
    project
  when: it is drafted
  then: later sentences open with links such as 'most recently', 'along the way' or 'this led to'
  quote: it doesnt link sentences when it could
  prompt: f53d9e6a6ca566f5
- id: EX-2
  given: a drafted summary whose sentences do not connect though they relate
  when: the voice reviewer reads it
  then: it reports a medium finding with the sentences to link
  quote: Make sure that it becomes the case in the future
  prompt: f53d9e6a6ca566f5
- id: EX-3
  given: a connector claiming a cause the evidence does not support
  when: the reviewer reads it
  then: it reports the unsupported connector
