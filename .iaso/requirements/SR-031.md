id: SR-031
title: The agent turns the owner's release edits into a reviewed revision
statement: When the owner says in plain words that they edited a release, the application-materials skill
  reads the cv regenerate --diff output, keeps each rewrite, carries out each instruction left as a note
  and removes it, asks about and records any new fact before use, runs cv regenerate, then runs the full
  review pipeline on the new revision and repairs its findings as for any draft, the owner's text included.
examples:
- id: EX-1
  given: r06/resume.md with a reworded bullet and a line asking to shorten the summary
  when: the owner tells the agent they edited r06
  then: r07 keeps the reworded bullet, has a shorter summary and no trace of the note, and its review
    angles are recorded
- id: EX-2
  given: an owner edit that adds a metric absent from the evidence
  when: the agent regenerates
  then: it asks the owner about the metric and records the answer before building
- id: EX-3
  given: a reviewer finding on a bullet the owner rewrote
  when: the review round ends
  then: the agent repairs the bullet and the repaired revision is reviewed again
