# Cover letter packet and independent delegation

## Actual inputs

Prefer a `cv build-application` bundle and run `cv verify-bundle --workspace ROOT --bundle outputs/SLUG/REVISION/bundle.json` before inspection. Review frozen inputs, checks and exact letter/resume artifacts together. Require actual motivation/support sources and prior answers in the explicit `--include` selection. Freshness verifies selected bytes only, never packet completeness, independent review or approval. Store review notes outside the immutable bundle.

Read canonical evidence and registry, selected projection, actual application answers with corrections, complete frozen JD and requirements, effective employer policy, current final resume, letter source, generated letter and exact check results. The final resume is a dependency, not an optional contextual summary. A changed resume makes prior letter consistency review stale.

Use explicit local authorized paths. Read actual source and artifacts rather than the author's summary. Record actual raw-byte hashes if the host can compute them; otherwise name the revision and state that exact-byte verification is unavailable. Keep candidate information local unless external disclosure is authorized. Source registry fingerprints do not authenticate underlying bytes.

## Independent assignments

Include the author's brief hiring argument, example-selection reasoning and motivation assessment/disposition with actual source references. First check employer policy, then read the letter and JD before opening that rationale. Record the contribution and supporting examples communicated by the letter alone; afterwards assess the rationale and full evidence. Missing rationale is a workflow gap, not evidence that the letter's claims are false.

An author delegates through real available host subagent tools. Separate contexts and actual tool evidence establish review independence; no invented reviewer IDs or staged “approved” responses. If tools are unavailable, perform a useful self-check and clearly label independence missing. One genuine reviewer may cover multiple angles, but each needs a separate verdict.

Suggested factual/consistency assignment:

> Independently review the letter against actual candidate evidence, answers and final resume. Do not edit. Break paragraphs into factual subclaims: identity, experience, skill, ownership, results and availability. Cite evidence/answer IDs for unsupported additions and contradictions. Preserve prototype/coursework/team limitations. The JD cannot prove applicant facts. Identify missing packet inputs and narrow repairs.

Suggested motivation/company assignment:

> Independently assess whether motivation comes from actual supplied answers and company facts come from actual checked sources. Connect supported contributions to concrete employer needs. Try changing only the company name to detect generic wording; do not cure genericness with invented admiration, passion or company familiarity. Unknown motivation remains provisional. Verify employer deliverables and AI-use restrictions.

Suggested persuasiveness/selection assignment:

> Read the letter and JD first, after employer-policy checks and before the author's rationale. State the hiring argument the letter communicates. Then compare its selection with the actual evidence and motivation disposition. Does each paragraph advance a concrete contribution to an employer need? Identify useful changes to examples, relevance, interpretation of supported outcomes or redundancy, with affected passages and narrow repairs. If none would help, explain why. Do not invent impact, demand new metrics, repeat answered/declined questions or manufacture criticism. Return a persuasiveness/selection verdict separate from factual correctness.

Suggested voice/comprehension assignment:

> Independently apply the [shared voice criteria](../../application-materials/references/voice-and-artifacts.md) and cover-letter fluency pass to the actual letter, private writing preferences and any supplied authentic samples. Read the whole letter before evaluating isolated sentences. Assess transitions, sentence rhythm and natural reading, especially whether motivation leads coherently into the proposed contribution. Identify exact abrupt, repetitive or overloaded passages and a narrow repair, or explain why no useful change is needed. Do not substitute generic connectors, invented causal history or forced stylistic variation. Check acronym comprehension without inventing answers. Return the existing voice/comprehension verdict with distinct fluency observations; a factual or persuasive pass is not a fluency verdict.

> Then check sentence-level meaning: use the JD/checked sources to distinguish each named company, team, product and project, and inspect the verbs, prepositions and pronouns around those names. Identify misleading wording and repair it narrowly without inventing relationships or banning ordinary phrases. Record which relationships and wording you checked, or that none occur. A user-reported wording defect must be reviewed afresh; unchanged bytes and an earlier no-findings verdict do not resolve it.

> Explicitly assess the final body paragraph through the closing and sign-off. Record whether the transition and final invitation or other appropriate ending feel natural and complete; cite an abrupt passage and narrow repair, or explain why it works. Do not introduce unsupported availability or insist on a fixed closing phrase, thank-you or length. Confirm the rendered ending and signature still fit cleanly after any repair.

Suggested visual/artifact assignment:

> Inspect the full exact rendered letter with actual visual tools, including every PDF page. Check clipping, font size, spacing, hierarchy, word/page limits, extraction order and source/artifact correspondence. Compare with current effective policy and actual `cv check` results. State uninspected artifacts/pages and tool limitations; source-only reading is not visual review.

## Review notes

This is a working report template, not an implemented production schema:

```text
Reviewer/run: actual tool-provided identity, or unavailable
Inspected inputs: actual paths, source/artifact revision observations
Final resume inspected: actual revision/hash, or missing
Factual/consistency verdict: no findings | findings | unreviewed
Persuasiveness/selection verdict: no findings | findings | unreviewed
Independent first reading: contribution and evidence communicated before rationale
Editorial opportunities: concrete changes, or reason no useful change is warranted
Motivation/company verdict: no findings | findings | unreviewed
Voice/comprehension verdict: no findings | findings | unreviewed
Fluency observations: transitions, sentence rhythm and natural reading; exact repairs or reason no change helps
Introduction observation: opening context, first sentence/sequence, handoff into evidence and supplied preferences; finding/repair or reason it works
Sentence-level meaning observation: named-entity relationships and wording checked (or absent); exact repair or reason appropriate
Closing observation: final body-to-conclusion transition, invitation or other ending, tone and sign-off; exact repair or reason complete
Visual/artifact verdict: no findings | findings | unreviewed
Findings: severity; paragraph/subclaim/page; source; narrow repair
Unknown motivation and other gaps: actual unanswered matters
Missing capability: actual limitation
```

Repair supported defects, regenerate a fresh revision and rerun affected reviewers/checks. Two repair/review rounds maximum; disclose remaining defects. Reusing a verdict for changed answers, policy, source, styles, rendered bytes or final resume is invalid. The host tracks staleness manually because authenticated closure and guarded release are not implemented. All artifacts remain previews; neither a genuine reviewer nor a scripted fixture authorizes submission.
