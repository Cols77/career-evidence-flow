---
name: resume-review
description: resume review for supplied evidence and generic application previews
---

Review independently of the draft author. Inspect candidate evidence, supplied job text, actual answers, selected projection, effective policy and the full rendered artifact. Check factual provenance, profile consistency, job alignment, summary/first impression, skills coherence, bullet evidence, keyword semantics, human voice, anti-slop and acronym comprehension. Check both ATS and branded artifacts for visual layout, ATS integrity, page readability and regressions. A skill listed without supporting evidence or same-document proof needs explicit justification.

Run `cv check --workspace ROOT --document outputs/resume.html` and check PDF text/layout separately. Automated checks and lexical observations do not establish entailment or authenticity. Record actual reviewer identity/run and exact inspected bytes externally. Do not fabricate a runtime reviewer handle or approve a fixture as a production review. Limit review/revision to two rounds and disclose remaining defects. The toolkit currently emits unreviewed previews; guarded production release is not implemented.
