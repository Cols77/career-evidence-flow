# Cover-letter argument and independent editorial review

Approved direction: strengthen the existing application and cover-letter review
workflows so factual correctness is assessed separately from the effectiveness
of the hiring argument. Keep existing commands and host orchestration.

Before drafting a requested letter, record a brief argument in private working
notes: the employer's main need, the candidate's supported contribution, the
best one or two examples, and the disposition of personal motivation. Reuse
answers; ask one focused motivation question when it would materially improve
the letter. Preserve unknown or declined motivation without inventing enthusiasm
or repeatedly interviewing the candidate.

The letter should explain the relevance of selected evidence, rather than
reproduce the resume's breadth. Keep technical mechanisms and outcomes that
advance that argument, along with necessary ownership and maturity boundaries.
Do not impose a universal word count, fixed paragraph template or success metric.

Independent reviewers first read the actual letter, job and employer policy and
record their initial assessment before reading the author's argument/rationale.
They then check the full evidence, answers and final resume. Add a distinct
persuasiveness verdict with concrete improvements or a reason no useful change
remains. Factual and visual passes do not establish a persuasive letter. A strong
letter needs no manufactured criticism. Retain exact-artifact visual inspection,
freshness checks and the existing two-round repair limit.

Author policy only in `skills/`, update the workflow documentation and README,
and regenerate host/package copies. No new CLI schema, scoring system, reviewer
service, hook gate or claimed automatic semantic enforcement is introduced.

Validate with independent fictional behavior exercises, existing installer/link
and distribution tests, the full test suite, generated-copy checks and an
independent consistency review. Real candidate drafts and review evidence remain
in the explicit ignored private workspace.
